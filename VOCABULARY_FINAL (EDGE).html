Vocabulary details
Data & accuracy

Main bank: the 23,119-entry common/JLPT subset from the open japanese-language-data project, derived primarily from JMdict. It intentionally excludes much of the rare/archaic/specialized full-JMdict firehose.

Meanings/readings/POS: JMdict-derived data. Examples: editor-curated JMdict-linked Tatoeba Japanese–English sentence pairs when available.

Frequency: OpenSubtitles-derived Japanese spoken/media frequency, matched against JMdict vocabulary. It is used as a usefulness signal, not as absolute truth.

JLPT labels: community-derived Waller classifications. The modern JLPT does not publish official fixed vocabulary lists; labels here are approximate study classifications.

Sentence readings: readings are stored in each example record at sync time as separate Japanese / Hiragana / English fields. They are not generated by a runtime reading engine. Any example with an empty reading, unresolved kanji, or incomplete furigana is rejected before it can appear in the app.

Dictionary data and derived dataset: CC BY-SA 4.0 / EDRDG-compatible licensing. Tatoeba examples retain their applicable Tatoeba licensing and attribution requirements.
