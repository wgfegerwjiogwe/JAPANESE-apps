Data, classification & attribution

Jōyō master set: 2,136 kanji from a KANJIDIC2-derived Jōyō dataset filtered to grades 1–6 and 8 for the 2010 revision. The app validates that the synced master data contains exactly 2,136 unique characters.

Readings / meanings / grade / frequency: KANJIDIC2, Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group (EDRDG), via the open japanese-language-data derived dataset.

Vocabulary: JMdict/EDICT-derived word entries supplied by kanjiapi.dev. Example words are ranked toward entries carrying common-word priority markers; the app does not invent vocabulary or readings.

Approximate JLPT: the synced dataset's N1–N5 study classifications (Jonathan Waller/Tanos-derived). The modern JLPT does not publish an official fixed kanji list, so these labels are shown only as approximate study levels.

School grade: grades 1–6 are elementary-school kyōiku kanji. Grade 8 means the remaining Jōyō kanji taught through secondary schooling.

EDRDG dictionary material is used under its published licence / CC BY-SA-compatible terms. This app includes attribution rather than claiming ownership of the underlying dictionary data.
